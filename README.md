# 28–0

A fan-made Overwatch esports draft game. Spin a team, roll an era, draft one player, and repeat until your lineup is full. Then your lineup plays a 28-match season (the length of the 2019 Overwatch League season) and the playoffs. Can you go 28–0?

**Play:** https://yorkedegg.github.io/28-0/

## How it works

- **Spins:** each spin rolls a team, then one of its eras. OWL teams have 2018–20 and 2021–23; OWCS teams have 2024–25. Every player who appeared for that team in those years is in the pool, rated at their best season there.
- **Formats:** 5v5 (Tank, Hitscan, Flex DPS, Main Support, Flex Support) or classic 6v6 (adds a Main Tank / Off-Tank split).
- **Modes:** a daily with the same spins for everyone, or free play.

## Where the data comes from

- **Rosters and team logos:** [Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/overwatch/) (CC BY-SA for text; logos belong to their teams).
- **OWL 2018–2022 ratings:** the official OWL Stats Lab exports. Per-10-minute stats are compared within each role, then combined with share of minutes played, team record and awards.
- **OWCS 2025 ratings:** per-map stats from [OWTV](https://owtv.gg), compared within each event.
- **OWCS 2024–25:** Player-of-the-Match awards and stage placements from [owtics](https://owtics.gg).
- **2023 and 2024:** these have no public stat files, so ratings use neighbouring seasons and team results, calibrated to the same scale as 2018–22.
- **Hitscan/flex and main/flex styles:** Stats Lab hero time, or Liquipedia signature heroes.

All ratings are estimates from that data, not official numbers.
